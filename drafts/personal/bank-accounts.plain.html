Common questions

How long does it take to open an account?

Most accounts can be opened in the app in around 10 minutes if you already bank with us, or a few days if you're switching from another bank.

Can I switch from another bank?

Yes, we support the Current Account Switch Service, which moves your payments and direct debits automatically within 7 working days.
